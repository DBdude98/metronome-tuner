Privacy Policy

Practice Desk · Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short: the app does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Microphone

The tuner uses the microphone to detect the pitch of your instrument. Audio is analysed in real time on your device only. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can turn microphone access off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, such as tempo, layout, themes and widget preferences, are saved locally on your iPad so the app and its widgets can use them. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Data we collect

None. The app has no servers, no third-party SDKs, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it is suitable for users of all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
